# Served names are identity
Frames the post house served keep their names and frame numbers exactly. Every frame we deliver goes out with the same names and numbers.
A clerical fix (for example a file named [108521-NNNNNN].dpx corrected to NNNNNN) is allowed only when recorded in
01_GOVERNANCE/change-log.csv with the original name, the new name, who and why.
Our own shot names (corrosion-056) may differ from their folder names (corrosion-052_3D). Both are written down in the inbox entry. Neither is changed to match the other.
